# Optical System — high

Lens, mount, focus, IR-cut. Optics decide what the sensor sees.

## Choosing

- **Focal length:** short (2.75–3.04 mm) = wide view, less detail;
  long (6 mm+) = narrow view, more detail. Match it to the
  distance of what you watch.
- **Mount:** M12 for compact cameras, CS/C-mount for
  interchangeable lenses (HQ Camera). C-mount needs a 5 mm adapter
  on a CS mount.
- **Focus:** fixed (set once), manual (screw), motorised autofocus
  (IMX708 Camera Module 3: 10 cm to infinity).
- **Image circle:** the lens must cover the sensor — a 1/3" lens
  vignettes on a 1/2.3" sensor.
- **IR-cut:** standard for day; NoIR (or a switchable IR-cut) for
  night. A switchable filter is the right answer for a day+night
  camera.

## Checks

No script checks optics — verify with a real image: corners sharp,
no vignetting, focus holds from the nearest to the farthest subject.
Focus drift with temperature is real on cheap mounts: test it
outdoors, not only on the bench.
