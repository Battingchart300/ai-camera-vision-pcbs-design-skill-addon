# Environmental / IP rating — high

The camera lives outside. The enclosure decides whether it survives
the year.

## IP ratings (IEC 60529)

- **IP54:** dust-protected, splashing water. Indoor / sheltered.
- **IP66:** dust-tight, powerful water jets. Rain-proof — the
  default for an outdoor camera.
- **IP67:** dust-tight, 1 m immersion for 30 min. For flooding risk.
- State the rating in the spec (`environment.ip_rating`).

## Rules

- Every cable entry sealed to the rating: glands, not holes.
- Condensation is the silent killer: a sealed box that breathes
  through a bad seal fogs the lens. A Gore vent or desiccant helps
  where the rating allows it.
- Operating range in the spec must cover the site: -20 °C winter
  nights and +60 °C summer sun inside the box are both real.
- UV: cheap plastic yellows and cracks in a year. Use UV-stable
  material or paint for a box that stays outside.

## Checks

No script checks the enclosure — verify by inspection: seals
seated, glands tight, no gap a wasp can enter.
